# Sky AutoMusic - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

## Who we are

Sky AutoMusic is developed by Artem Zhukov. It is a fan-made app, not affiliated with thatgamecompany.
Questions about privacy: zhukovartemvl@gmail.com.

## What stays on your device

Your melodies, playlists, calibration and settings are kept on your device. The app has no accounts and doesn't
upload your files; they are not in Android's backup and don't move with it to a new phone. To take your melodies to
another device, export them.

## Accessibility service

The Android app uses the Accessibility Service API only to tap the notes of a melody in the game, to know which
app and which kind of window is open, so playback pauses when you leave the game (the volume panel and game panels
over the game don't count), and, while a melody plays, to pause it on a press of a volume button: the service then
sees presses of the device's buttons, the app reacts to the volume buttons only and the press still changes the
volume. It doesn't read what is on the screen or what you type. Nothing the app gets through the service leaves your
device.

## Usage statistics

To fix bugs and decide what to build next, the Android app sends usage statistics through Google Firebase
Analytics:

- which screens are opened and which features are used, such as import, export, playback, the editor, the overlay
  and calibration, and how long the app is used;
- roughly how many melodies are in your library;
- the screen size and where you placed the note grid, to improve automatic calibration;
- settings such as theme, language and pause triggers;
- the device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate location (country, region, city), which
  Firebase works out from the IP address.

The statistics are tied to a random app instance ID, not to your name, email or any account. You can turn them off
in the app: the tab with the ⓘ icon -> Privacy, the switch at the end of the page.

## Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a report through Google Firebase Crashlytics: the error
and where in the code it happened, the device model and Android version, and the state of the app at that moment:
the open screen, the number of melodies, the calibration profile, whether the accessibility service is on, the last
actions. A report is tied to a random installation ID. We don't add the contents of
your files to a report. To tell how often the app crashes, Crashlytics also counts app launches: each launch sends a
short record with its time, the same installation ID, the device model, Android version and app version. Crash
reports and these records are sent even with statistics turned off: without them we can't fix crashes.

## Logs you send

The app keeps a log of what it does on your device: its start, the screens, the accessibility service, the overlay,
playback, import and export, errors. The log has no names of your melodies or files. It never leaves your device by
itself: it is sent only when you tap "Send logs" and choose where to send it, with the app version, the device model,
the Android version, the app's permissions and the number of melodies and playlists.

## Who gets the data

The data is processed by Google as the provider of Firebase
([Firebase privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
We don't sell data, don't show ads and don't share data with anyone else.

## How long it is kept

Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Analytics event data is kept for no longer than 14 months; totals in reports
can stay longer.

## Children

The app doesn't ask anyone, including children, for names, emails or other contact details.

## Your choices

Turn statistics off with the switch in the app. Uninstalling the app removes what it stored on your device.
The data sent is not tied to your name or an account, so we can't find which of it is yours; events and crash
reports are removed on their own after the periods above. For questions about your data, write to zhukovartemvl@gmail.com.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears here and in the app with a new date.
